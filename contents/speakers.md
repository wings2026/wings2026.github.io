    <h3>Julie Desjardins, University of Toronto Mississauga</h3>
    <p>Julie Desjardins received her PhD from Université Paris Diderot in 2016. After a year at Université Grenoble Alpes and another 9 months at the Max Planck Institute in Bonn, Julie obtained a limited-term contract as an assistant professor at the University of Toronto Mississauga – which eventually was followed by a tenure-track position at the same university in 2023. It started two weeks after the birth of her second child – just on time for a year long maternity leave. Her research is mainly concerned with the variation of the rank or the root number of elliptic curves in families, and rational points on del Pezzo surfaces. When she finally finds a bit of quiet, Julie writes sci-fi novels in French.</p>
